One-line office joke for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open" — a single line, at most 200 characters, judged head to head by an AI judge. The entry:

> I asked IT for a second monitor. They gave me a second ticket number.

69 characters, chosen by a 28-pair round-robin tournament against the judge's published criteria (8 original candidates; this one went 7–0).

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` outside the grader).
